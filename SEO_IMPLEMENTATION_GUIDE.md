### Step 1: Google Search Console Setup
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/)
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter your domain: `cbdpanelandpaint.co.nz`
4. Verify ownership using HTML tag method or DNS verification

### Step 2: Submit Sitemap
Once verified:
1. In Google Search Console, go to "Sitemaps" (left menu)
2. Add new sitemap URL: `https://cbdpanelandpaint.co.nz/sitemap.xml`
3. Click "Submit"

### Step 3: Request Indexing
1. In Google Search Console, go to "URL Inspection"
2. Enter: `https://cbdpanelandpaint.co.nz`
3. Click "Request Indexing"
4. Repeat for key pages:
   - `/panel-beating`
   - `/mechanical`
   - `/caravans-boats`
   - `/contact`

   - Phone number
   - Business hours
   - Photos of your workshop
   - Customer reviews

1. Go to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/)
