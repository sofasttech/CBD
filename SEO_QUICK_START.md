## 📝 Step-by-Step SEO Submission (30 minutes)

### Step 1: Google Search Console (10 min)
1. ✅ Go to https://search.google.com/search-console/
2. ✅ Click "Start Now" and sign in with Google
3. ✅ Select "URL prefix" and enter: `https://cbdpanelandpaint.co.nz`
4. ✅ Choose "HTML tag" verification method
5. ✅ Copy the meta tag they give you
6. ✅ Add it to your `index.html` in the `<head>` section
7. ✅ Deploy your site
8. ✅ Click "Verify" in Google Search Console

### Step 2: Submit Sitemap (2 min)
1. ✅ In Google Search Console, click "Sitemaps" (left menu)
2. ✅ Enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. ✅ Click "Submit"
4. ✅ Wait for "Success" status (may take a few minutes)

### Step 3: Request Indexing (5 min)
1. ✅ Click "URL Inspection" (left menu)
2. ✅ Enter: `https://cbdpanelandpaint.co.nz`
3. ✅ Click "Request Indexing"
4. ✅ Repeat for:
   - `https://cbdpanelandpaint.co.nz/panel-beating`
   - `https://cbdpanelandpaint.co.nz/mechanical`
   - `https://cbdpanelandpaint.co.nz/contact`

### Step 4: Google Business Profile (10 min)
1. ✅ Go to https://www.google.com/business/
2. ✅ Search for "CBD Panel and Paint"
3. ✅ If found, claim it. If not, create new listing
4. ✅ Add:
   - Business name
   - Category: "Auto Body Shop"
   - Address
   - Phone
   - Website: `https://cbdpanelandpaint.co.nz`
   - Hours of operation
   - Upload 5-10 photos of your shop

### Step 5: Bing Webmaster Tools (5 min)
1. ✅ Go to https://www.bing.com/webmasters/
2. ✅ Sign in and add your site
3. ✅ Import settings from Google Search Console (easiest)
4. ✅ Submit sitemap: `https://cbdpanelandpaint.co.nz/sitemap.xml`
